Privacy Policy – For Little Learners and Their Grown-ups

📅 Effective Date

November 8, 2026

This policy explains how Tiny Genius and its advertising service handle information.

🧸 About Tiny Genius

Tiny Genius is an educational learning app designed for young children. It includes learning cards, quizzes and interactive activities.

📚 Information Handled by the App

Tiny Genius does not currently provide user accounts or sign-in. Children are not asked to enter their names, email addresses, phone numbers or location.

Learning progress, quiz answers and scores are kept temporarily in app memory. They are not currently saved to an account or app database.

The app creates a short sound file in its private cache for sound effects.

📢 Google AdMob Advertising

Tiny Genius displays Google Mobile Ads banner advertisements on the home screen.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share information such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

For further details, visit Google's Privacy Policy.

🛡️ Children's Privacy and Advertising

Tiny Genius is designed for children. Its ad requests are configured for child-directed treatment and a maximum ad content rating of G.

The app also removes the Android advertising ID permission from its manifest.

These settings provide child-directed advertising protections. However, they do not mean that the advertising SDK processes no information.

For details, see Google's Mobile Ads SDK Data Disclosure.

🔗 Information Sharing

Tiny Genius does not operate its own user-account or advertising server.

Information handled by Google Mobile Ads SDK may be shared with Google as described in this policy.

If someone uses the Share App feature, Android opens its system share chooser. The selected sharing application handles the shared content under its own privacy practices.

🔐 Security

Tiny Genius does not maintain its own user-data server. Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Google controls the security practices for information processed through its SDK.

🗑️ Data Retention and Deletion

Learning and quiz state is temporary and remains in memory while the app is in use. The generated sound file is stored in app cache and may be cleared by Android.

Uninstalling the app removes its local app data. Tiny Genius does not control how long Google retains information processed by its advertising SDK. Please consult Google's policies for details.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parents and Guardians

Parents or guardians can contact the developer with questions or privacy requests concerning a child's use of Tiny Genius.
